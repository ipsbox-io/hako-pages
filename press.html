One-liner

Hako is a capture-first app for notes and todos — for people who forget things before they get around to organizing them. iPhone · iPad · Mac, iCloud sync.

Hako は「整理する前に忘れてしまう人」のための、まず放り込むメモ＆ToDoアプリ。iPhone・iPad・Mac、iCloud同期。

Facts

  • Name: Hako（ハコ）
  • Developer: BOX LLC — Yokohama, Japan (solo developer)
  • Platforms: iPhone / iPad / Mac (native SwiftUI)
  • Price: Free download + Hako Pro one-time purchase (launch price ¥1,000 JP — local pricing per App Store). No subscription.
  • Privacy: Data Not Collected. No account. iCloud private database only.
  • Three spaces: Inbox (capture) · Note (write) · ToDo (act)
  • App Store: apps.apple.com/app/hako-notes-todos/id6787610465
